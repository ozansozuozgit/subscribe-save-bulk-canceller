How it works
  1. Pick what to keep. On your Subscribe & Save page, open the small panel in the corner. Everything starts as Keep; click an item to switch it to Cancel.
  2. Review and confirm. You see exactly what will be cancelled and kept before anything happens.
  3. It cancels the rest, one at a time. At a human pace, using Amazon’s own Cancel button. You can pause anytime.
Backup & restore
Your choices and history stay in this browser. A backup file contains product names and subscription IDs, so keep it private.
